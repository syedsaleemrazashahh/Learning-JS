Set Clear Goal

Ap k har ik din k goal hone chahiye. Yeh goals achievable hone chahiye, taaki aap apne progress acha kar sakhte aur aus kam mein maharat hassil kar sakhte.

Take Break

Regular breaks lena zaroori hai taaki aapki energy aur focus bana rahe. Har 60 minutes ke baad ek chhota break lena behtar hai aapko fresh rakhne k liye.

Create a ToDo list

Daily to-do list banayein, taki aap apne tasks ko track kar sakein aur koi bhi zaroori kaam miss na ho.

Self-Refelction

Din ke end mein apne time management ka review karein aur dekhein ki kya behtar ho sakta tha.

Distractions

Apne kaam ke doran distractions ko kam se kam rakhne ki koshish karein, jaise phone ka istamaal sirf zaroorat par karein.
